Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Scripture by Hand is designed to keep your reading and writing practice on your device. The app does not collect or transmit personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your selected passage, bookmarks, reading preferences, and any Bible text you import are stored locally on your device. Imported text stays in the app’s local storage and is not uploaded. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove this information.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily writing reminder, iOS schedules a local notification at the time you choose. The reminder is handled on your device. No reminder data is sent to a server.

Data collection and sharing

The app has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Its reading and writing features do not send your passage, bookmarks, preferences, imports, or reminder settings to our servers. Scripture text and app resources are included with the app so the core reading experience can work offline.

External links

If you choose to open a translation source or the project issue tracker, that destination’s own privacy policy applies.

Contact

For privacy questions or to report an issue, use the project issue tracker. Issues are public, so do not include private or sensitive information.
